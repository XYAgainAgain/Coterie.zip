## perks

### A Warlock's Services
**When you use Blood Sorcery or occult expertise to solve a problem for someone outside your Coterie**, you automatically create a Debt over them. Coterie members start each owing you 1 Debt for services "freely" rendered.

### Arbiter's Eyes
**When you *Discern Vibes* or *Catch the Scent* to determine if someone has recently committed a crime or transgression**, you have Advantage on the roll.

### Arcane Appraisal
**You can identify the presence and general nature of supernatural effects, Blood Sorcery, active Disciplines, wards, and curses without rolling.** Specifics (who, when, parameters) require **Catch the Scent** or Discipline use.

### Barely a Beast
**You never Frenzy from Hunger alone.** At 5 Hunger, retain full control but have Disadvantage on all rolls except **Hunt** and **Feed**. Supernatural effects and Discipline Powers can still provoke Frenzy.

### Beast's Leash
**When the Beast rises due to Hunger or mundane effects**, you can choose to Frenzy voluntarily instead of **Staying Chill**. You retain control: choose your target, avoid Harming your Coterie, and remember everything. Bane penalty is only -1 regardless of BP. Afterwards, gain +1 Hunger (max 4).

### Borrowed Time
**When you consume Vitae from your patron** (they gain +1 Hunger, you slake normally), choose: heal Sup. or Agg. Harm equal to {{patron-bp}}, or gain Advantage on {{patron-bp}} Discipline rolls until you sleep.

### Brutality
**When you inflict Harm through hands-on violence** (fists, claws, melee weapons, thrown objects, etc.), add {{bp@min:1}} to the Harm dealt.

### Center of Gravity
**When you are the focus of attention in a social scene**, all non-Coterie characters (except Elders) take -1 Ongoing to notice anything that isn't you. No roll needed; lasts as long as you're actively engaging the crowd.

### Death's Confidant
**When you encounter a corpse or sense a ghost's presence**, ask it 1 question and receive a truthful (if cryptic) answer without rolling. Anything beyond requires Discipline use.

### Duskborn
**You can walk in sunlight** (take 1 Sup. Harm/scene of sustained exposure; reducible). You always count as having **Blush of Life** active at no cost. You can eat, drink, use touchscreens, and pass medical exams.

### Face of Horror
**When you deliberately reveal your true form to intimidate or terrify**, roll **Influence** with Advantage using your highest stat; ignore your Bane penalty for that roll. Unprepared mortals flee or freeze.

### Gift of Apis
**Animal blood nourishes you as effectively as human blood regardless of BP.** Ignore all BP-related **Feeding** restrictions from animals. Animals never have Resonance. You may select the Farmer Predator Type at any BP.

### In Harmony
**1/Scene, when you successfully use any Melpominee Power**, one ally who can hear you takes +1 Forward. If other Daughters participate, bonus extends to all allies who hear you; you take +1 Ongoing to Melpominee per participating Daughter (max +3) until you stop/scene ends.

### Indomitable
**When you *Stay Chill* to resist supernatural coercion or commands from other Kindred**, roll with Advantage.

### Infectious Insight
**When you share info from *Discern Vibes* or *Catch the Scent* with a Coterie member and they act on it**, they take +1 Forward.

### Just a Taste
**When you *Feed* from a vampire**, temporarily gain 1 of their Disciplines (your choice), its Perk, and all its level 1 Powers. Lasts until you slumber or reach 5 Hunger.

### Living Rampart
**When you *Protect the Coterie***, treat a **6-** as a **7–9**. You ignore the 1/Scene usage limit. On **7–9**, protect yourself plus one ally within Close Range.

### Lucky Devil
**1/Session, you can reroll any single roll you've just made**, and take either result.

### Mending Touch
**1/Scene, heal 2 Sup. or 1 Agg. Harm on a willing creature via physical contact** (no roll). Sup. costs +1 Hunger; Agg. costs +2 Hunger.

### Muse's Gift
**1/Session, you can spend a quiet moment with a willing character to inspire them.** They gain Advantage on their next roll aligned with the emotional tone. If it's a Coterie member and the roll succeeds, you both gain +1 XP.

### Phantom Trick
You can conjure brief, minor sensory illusions at will (wrong-direction sound, peripheral movement, phantom scent). **When you weave one into any Move as a distraction**, take +1 Forward. They dissolve under direct scrutiny.

### Pillar of Osiris
**You can designate a location as your Pillar** (1 full night of meditation). While there: +1 Ongoing to Bardo Powers and **Stay Chill**. Meditating for 1 full scene removes 1 Stain. Must return 1/week; each week away, take cumulative -1 Ongoing to **Stay Chill** (reset by meditating for 1 scene).

### Playing Possum
**When another Kindred dismisses you as beneath notice or harmless**, immediately ask the Storyteller 1 question from **Discern Vibes** or **Catch the Scent** about them without rolling or spending Hold.

### Provider of Last Resort
**When you give someone something they genuinely need and can't easily get elsewhere**, you automatically gain a Debt over them (they may not know).

### Psychic Predator
**You are not a vampire.** You **Feed** on ephemera from mortals and spirits (not Kindred). Walk in daylight, pass as human, never Frenzy (unless forced by Powers). Cannot eat/drink or use **Blush of Life**. You can roll +Wits wherever +Blood is called for. At 5 Hunger you fade: lose Discipline access & have Disadvantage on all rolls except **Hunt** and **Feed**.

### Pulling Strings
**When you *Influence* someone you have a Debt with** (in either direction), or a mortal while you're both in darkness, roll with Advantage.

### Resonant Voice
**When you *Influence* someone through direct speech, song, or vocalization**, treat a **6-** as a **7–9**.

### Righteous Hunger
**When you *Feed* from someone you believe genuinely deserves punishment**, slake 1 additional Hunger. If you successfully Diablerize them, lose no Humanity and clear all Stains.

### Sanguine Sommelier
**When you *Influence* a mortal's emotional state**, you can shift their Blood Resonance to match the emotion you're cultivating (takes a few minutes). **When a Kindred *Feeds* from that mortal**, they get the Resonance you set.

### Scrappy
**You cannot have a negative modifier to any roll for any reason other than Hunger.** The worst you can do is add nothing.

### Scrupulous Sentinel
**When you are within your Haven or with your treasure and acting to defend/maintain/control it**, treat any **6-** as a **7–9**. Does not apply elsewhere, even if you carry your treasure with you.

### Sculptor's Claws
You can reshape your own physical features (skin, bone, coloring, height, build) over an uninterrupted scene without rolling. Changes are permanent until reshaped. **When you reveal a monstrous alteration to intimidate**, roll **Influence** with Advantage.

### Serpent's Tongue
**When you *Influence* someone by appealing to a desire/vice/need they're suppressing**, treat a **6-** as a **7–9**.

### Shadow's Embrace
**When you *Slip Away* and shadows are available**, treat a **6-** as a **7–9**.

### Sharpened Senses
**You always sense mundane and supernatural creatures within Close Range** (even in darkness/while blinded) without rolling. **When you *Catch the Scent* to track prey or detect danger**, roll with Advantage.

### Soul Sight
**When you *Discern Vibes* on an entity**, you may ask "What is their current Humanity?", "Do they intend to harm me?", or "Are they concealing their true nature?" without spending Hold.

### Still Kicking
**Your BP is 0 and you're alive.** Heartbeat, body heat, digestion, sunlight, pass medical exams. No **Blush of Life** needed (or possible). Must be nice.

### Stone Hide
**You have always-active {{bp@min:1}}-Armor.** Unlike other Armor, this also reduces Agg. Harm.

### Tactical Command
**1/Scene, when you give a direct order to an ally in a tense situation and they follow it**, they gain Advantage and +1 Forward on the roll to execute it.

### The Cobweb
**1/Session, you can ask the Storyteller a single yes-or-no question about anything in the past, present, or imminent future**. The answer is always truthful but arrives filtered through your affliction (visions, whispers, sensations).

### The Warrens
**When you *Catch the Scent* regarding anything happening in your territory**, treat a **6-** as a **7–9**.

### Unshackled Servant
**You have no Beast, no Pred. Type, and never Frenzy** (unless forced by Powers). **Stay Chill** still applies for stress/coercion, never for rampage. At 5 Hunger, enter withdrawal: lose Discipline access & have Disadvantage on all rolls except **Feed**.

### Unyielding Privilege
**When you *Stay Chill* to resist coercion, mental domination, or intimidation from any source**, treat a **6-** as a **7–9**.

### Wages of Pain
**When you successfully *Feed* from a living vessel**, slake 1 additional Hunger. This can drop you below the Hunger floor imposed by high BP.

## banes

### Adversarial Aesthetics
**When your surroundings are aesthetically hostile** (filthy, institutional, very bland), take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all Resolve and Discipline rolls. Storyteller determines based on your personal aesthetic.

### Agonizing Empathy
**Whenever your *Feeding* causes Harm to the vessel**, you suffer the same amount as Agg. Harm, up to {{bp@min:1}} max. Manifests as vivid internal bruising at the bite location.

### Agonizing Kiss
**When you *Feed* from a living vessel**, your Kiss is agony, not ecstasy. Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to **Feed** rolls unless vessel is restrained/unconscious/willing. All living vessels you **Feed** from take 1 Sup. Harm regardless.

### Asceticism
**When your Hunger is ≤2**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all Discipline rolls. Only starvation unlocks your Vitae's full potential. (Third eye still bleeds & you're still delicious, though.)

### Blood Bound
**You are Blood Bound to your patron.** They can Cash In your Debt {{patron-bp}} times without depleting it, and Invoke it {{patron-bp}} times per night. Cannot be refused/erased/transferred. Ends only via Embrace or patron's Final Death.

### Built to Serve
**You cannot pass as mortal.** Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to social rolls with unfamiliar/hostile characters who see you (stationary in architecture = assumed statue). Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to **Stay Chill** vs. mental coercion (-{{bp*2}} vs. Tremere). Humanity cap: 6.

### Burning Blood
**When you witness a direct violation of your Convictions**, immediately **Stay Chill** at -{{bp@min:1}}. On **6-**, enter a furious Frenzy targeting the source; can't stop until they're incapacitated/dead/driven off or you're restrained.

### Callousness
**When you make a Remorse Check**, roll with Disadvantage. The Beast eats your guilt before you can feel it.

### Cold-Blooded
You can only use **Blush of Life** if you've **Fed** from a living vessel in the current or previous scene. **When you do**, make {{bp@min:1}} Hunger Checks instead of 1. If any would bring you to 5 Hunger, **Blush of Life** fails and you stabilize at 4.

### Cursed Courtesy
**You cannot willingly enter a private residence unless invited.** Forcing entry: gain +1 Hunger and take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all Discipline rolls for the duration. Public/abandoned buildings unaffected; apartments count as separate residences. Cannot take the *Invitations* Folkloric Bane.

### Decay
**When you move into your Coterie's Haven**, distribute -1 to Coterie stats for each Touchstone you have (no stat below -3). If a Touchstone visits the Haven, lose them permanently and mark 1 Stain, but remove that -1 penalty. New Touchstones during play assign a new -1 immediately.

### Defanged Blood
**Your Vitae cannot Blood Bond other Kindred.** You can still be Bound by others. Can still Blood Bond mortals/Ghouls/animals, but requires {{bp@min:1}} additional **Feedings** beyond the normal 3.

### Denied Beast
**When you Frenzy**, the starved Beast erupts. Take -{{osirian-penalty}} Ongoing to **Stay Chill** for the rest of the night. You also cannot Embrace anyone; attempts simply kill the mortal.

### Diablerist's Thirst
**When you *Feed* from another vampire**, you must **Stay Chill** or enter a feeding Frenzy and try to drain them. **7+** to resist, then take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to continue resisting (3 successes ends it).

### Diluted & Disliked
**Your BP is 0 and cannot increase** (except via Diablerie). You take Agg. Harm from blades/bullets/sources that are only Sup. for full Kindred. Cannot create Blood Bonds. Embrace attempts usually kill the mortal. Storyteller may apply -1 to -2 Forward on social rolls with Kindred.

### Distorted Image
**When you interact with technology or electronic devices**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing. Mirrors warp, cameras distort, surveillance corrupts. At BP 3+, touchscreens and voice-activated devices are unusable; **Blush of Life** doesn't help.

### Doomed to Wander
**You cannot sleep in the same location more than 1/week** (1+ mile apart to count). Violating this: take 1 Agg. Harm (2 at BP 3+). Applies in Torpor. Mobile Havens are safe if parked differently each day.

### Dragon's Tether
**You must slumber within/around your treasure each day.** Resting away: take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to Resolve rolls until you return. Cumulative per consecutive day away (max -{{bp*2}}).

### Fractured Perception
**You have a supernatural affliction** (defined at creation). When the Beast presses in (Hunger 4+, Frenzy, **6-** on **Stay Chill**, etc.), take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to **Discern Vibes** and **Catch the Scent** until pressure eases/scene ends. Storyteller may introduce 1 false detail while active that you believe is true.

### Hierarchy
**When you use a Discipline Power against a Kindred with higher BP than yours**, roll with Disadvantage. To physically attack such a Kindred, first **Stay Chill** or find yourself unable to raise a hand. Failure means no violence against them for the scene (unless they start it).

### Holy Symbols
**Being within 2 Ranges of a holy symbol** (you select which apply) deals 2 Agg. Harm/round. Within 1 Range: 3-Harm. Physical contact: 5-Harm. (+3 XP)

### Holy Water
Touching holy water deals 1 Agg. Harm. Drinking it causes Final Death. Why would you do that? (+1 XP)

### Hunted Blood
**You have a third eye on your forehead**; physical coverings work, no supernatural Power can hide it. Opens and weeps blood when you use Active Powers. Nearby Kindred at 4+ Hunger must **Stay Chill** or try to **Feed** from you. Non-Salubri who taste your blood must **Stay Chill** at -{{bp@min:1}} or keep going; Blood Leeches have Disadvantage.

### Infestation
Vermin follow you constantly and infest any enclosed space you inhabit. Everyone in your Haven (including you) takes -2 Ongoing to social rolls & anything requiring patience/focus. At other enclosed locations where you spend ≥1 scene, -1 Ongoing to the same. Animalism attempts on the vermin have Disadvantage.

### Inner Song
**You always hear music no one else can.** Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all Wits rolls (always active). In loud/overstimulating environments, also applies to **Stay Chill**. In total silence, **Stay Chill** or trigger Compulsion. On any **12+** Wits roll, penalty doesn't apply.

### Invitations
**You are physically unable to enter any private building** unless invited by someone with sufficient authority. (+2 XP)

### Lacking Lineage
**You carry no supernatural curse, but Kindred society stigmatizes you.** Storyteller may apply -1 to -2 Forward on Demeanor rolls with Kindred who know. Discipline XP costs: +{{bp@min:1}} to unlock access; +{{(bp/2)@roundup}} for non-starter Powers.

### Mark of the Beast
**When you Frenzy**, your body gains {{bp@min:1}} bestial features. Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to social rolls with unfamiliar characters until you sleep. Cannot be concealed by mundane means; doesn't stack from multiple Frenzies per night.

### Monstrous Visage
**You cannot pass as mortal by any usual means.** Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to social rolls with characters who see your true form and are unfamiliar/hostile. Also applies to supernatural disguises (Obfuscate, etc.).

### Noxious Blood
**Your Vitae is poison**. Any creature who drinks it takes {{bp@min:1}} Agg. Harm per instance. Your Vitae cannot heal mortal injuries. Creating a Ghoul or Blood Bond requires the mortal to survive this damage each time they drink (most don't).

### Noxious Garlic
Touching garlic deals 1 Agg. Harm. Eating it deals 3 Agg. Harm but sets Hunger to 0 and prevents **Feeding** until next night. (+1 XP)

### Pale Beasts
**When you see an all-white animal**, **Stay Chill** with Disadvantage or flee the scene at once. (+1 XP)

### Rarefied Palate
**You have an exclusive *Feeding* preference**, and blood outside it provides zero sustenance. Roll +Wits in close proximity to sense a match (**7+** = certain). At BP 3+, restriction narrows further (define with Storyteller).

### Running Water
**You are physically unable to cross running water** (streams, rivers, sewer pipes, aqueducts, garden hoses). Bodies of water (ponds, lakes, oceans) don't trigger this. (+2 XP)

### Searing Silver
**Touching silver** deals 2 Agg. Harm. If incorporated into a weapon, all its damage is Agg. (+2 XP)

### Severe Light Allergy
**When you are exposed to bright, direct light** (spotlights, high beams, harsh fluorescents), take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls. Sunlight deals +{{bp}} extra Agg. Harm on top of normal effects. Candles/dim lamps/ambient glow don't trigger this.

### Stake Bait
**Being staked through the heart** causes instant Final Death instead of incapacitation. Oops! (+3 XP)

### Stigmata
**When at ≥4 Hunger**, you bleed from wounds on your hands, feet, and forehead. They never heal. While bleeding: Disadvantage on all Demeanor rolls and a walking Masquerade violation. (+3 XP)

### Stolen Blood
**When you Blood Surge**, make {{bp@min:1}} additional Hunger Checks on top of the normal one. If any would bring you to 5 Hunger, choose to cancel the Surge (keep gained Hunger, max 4) or complete it and immediately Frenzy at 5.

### Survival Instincts
**When you *Stay Chill* against fear** (fire, sunlight, genuine survival threats), roll with Disadvantage. Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls until you are guaranteed safe from whatever triggered it. Your Beast would rather run than fight.

### The Wasting
**When you *Feed* from the same mortal more than once**, start a 6-segment Wasting Clock. Each **Feeding** advances {{1+bp}} segments. Fades 1 segment/week of no contact. Full Clock = their decline becomes impossible to hide.

### Unbirth Name
You have a secret mortal name you cannot forget. Anyone who learns it gains a Debt over you. **If they speak it to your face**, they gain Advantage to resist your Discipline Powers for the scene, and you have Disadvantage to resist theirs.

### Unnatural Manifestations
**When you use any Discipline Power in the presence of mortals**, they're deeply unsettled for the scene. Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to social rolls with those mortals (except intimidation). Kindred recognize you as a vampire but suffer no ill effects.

### UV Light
**When exposed to UV light from sources other than the sun**, take 3 Agg. Harm/round of contact. (+3 XP)

### Violence
**When you have ≥3 Hunger and deal Harm to anyone**, the Beast demands you slay them. You must keep attacking until they're dead or you successfully **Stay Chill**. Killing this way marks 2 Stains (unless aligned with Convictions).

## compulsions

### Affective Empathy
**When you perceive genuine suffering in someone nearby**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to helping them. Ends when you meaningfully alleviate their pain/a more immediate crisis supersedes/scene ends.

### Arrogance
**When a situation seems leaderless or poorly handled**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to establishing yourself as the authority. Ends when someone obeys a genuine direct order from you (no technicalities)/scene ends.

### Cacophony
**When surrounding sounds clash with your Inner Song**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to performing or using Melpominee. Ends when the sonic environment aligns with your Inner Song/scene ends.

### Covetousness
**When you become aware of something valuable or desirable to you in the scene**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to acquiring it. Ends when you establish ownership/it becomes unattainable/scene ends.

### Cryptophilia
**When you suspect someone nearby is hiding a secret**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to uncovering information. Ends when you learn a secret significant enough to be useful/scene ends.

### Dark Devotion
**When your patron expresses displeasure or your position feels threatened**, take -{{patron-bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not working toward earning their approval. Ends when you perform meaningful service/threat passes.

### Delusion
**When you catch a detail or pattern others overlook**, you become convinced it's the most important thing happening. Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to chasing it until you investigate to your satisfaction/scene ends.

### Feelings Fixation
**When you sense intense emotion from someone nearby**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not working toward reaching them to **Feed**. Ends when you make prolonged contact/scene ends.

### Feral Impulses
**When someone directly challenges your strength or competence in front of others**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls involving reason, logic, or social grace. Ends when you physically establish dominance/scene ends.

### Intervention
**When you witness cruelty or someone succumbing to base urges**, take -{{osirian-penalty}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to peaceful intervention. Violence to resolve this marks 1 Stain. Ends when you de-escalate/scene ends.

### Judgment
**When someone violates one of your Convictions/personal codes in your presence**, you must drink their blood as punishment. Until you do/scene ends, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls except ones made to punish them.

### Morbidity
**When you encounter death or the boundary between it and life**, you must transform something's state (kill, resurrect, conclude, rekindle). Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all other rolls until you do/scene ends.

### Obsession
**When something of extraordinary beauty captures your attention**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls unrelated to the fixation. If someone redirects you, **Stay Chill** or become entranced (can't act until physically snapped out). Ends when you can't perceive it/scene ends.

### Perfectionism
**When you fail to meet your own standards**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls until you achieve a **12+** on any roll/scene ends. Repeating a previously-attempted action reduces the penalty by 1 (from BP → 0).

### Rebellion
**When you feel under authority/control you didn't agree to**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not working toward defiance. Ends when you meaningfully rebel/scene ends.

### Ruthlessness
**When you fail any roll (**6-**)**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls except pursuing the failed objective. Ends when you succeed/it becomes impossible/scene ends.

### Tempting Fate
**When a problem has both a safe and risky solution**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to cautious/conventional rolls. Committing to the riskiest option ends it; take +1 Ongoing until your plan resolves.

### The Vigil
**When you perceive a direct threat to your Haven, Flock, or someone you've sworn to protect**, take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not dedicated to defending your charge. You cannot voluntarily retreat while active. Ends when you interpose yourself/neutralize the danger/scene ends.

### Transgression
**When you recognize a taboo or moral boundary someone holds sacred**, you must test it. Take -{{bp@min:1}} Ongoing to all rolls not working toward enticing someone into violating a Conviction (causing 1+ Stain) or personal tenet/scene ends.
