### *Focus Shift (Discipline Perk)*

**When you spend a scene meditating with a willing ally at your Pillar**, you or your ally may exchange one purchased Discipline Power for a different Power of the same level from the same Discipline. The change takes hold after your next slumber.

### *Exuberance (from V5 Potence L3, Tattered Facade)*

V5 source: Passive. Treat Potence rating as 2 higher (can exceed 5), but on any critical or failure, take 1 Aggravated Harm. Relocated here because the "exceed your limits at a physical cost" fantasy fits Bardo's transcendence-through-sacrifice theme better than Potence's raw-force identity. Needs full Coterie conversion.
